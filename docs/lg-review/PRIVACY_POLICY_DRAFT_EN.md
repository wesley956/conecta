# RonecaPlayTV Privacy Policy — Draft

**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`

RonecaPlayTV is operated by `[SELLER LEGAL NAME]` (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains how the RonecaPlayTV television application and its activation services process information.

## Information processed

The service may process:

- a generated television activation code;
- a device installation identifier and installation credential;
- television platform, application version, and last-seen time;
- account/customer label, subscription status, and expiration date;
- playback diagnostics such as content label, content type, playback position, error category, retry count, and recovery result;
- IP address and security logs needed to protect the service.

We do not need the user’s precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier for normal operation.

## Purposes

Information is used to authenticate an installation, activate authorized access, deliver the configured catalog, preserve favorites and viewing progress, provide support, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

## Content catalogs

RonecaPlayTV is a player and activation platform. It does not include a general commercial media library. Catalog access is configured by an authorized account provider. Review accounts receive a separate demonstration catalog.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly to host the application, authentication, storage, diagnostics, and network delivery, or disclosed when legally required.

## Retention

Account and device records are retained while the service relationship is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, support, accounting, and legal obligations. Diagnostic records may be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

## Security

We use transport encryption, per-installation credentials, restricted server-side access, role separation, limited review accounts, and audit logs. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## User choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing by contacting `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`.

## Children

The service is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Account providers are responsible for content suitability and lawful account administration.

## Changes

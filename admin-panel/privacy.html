RonecaPlayTV Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

RonecaPlayTV (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a television media-player and controlled device-activation service. This policy explains the information processed by the television application, activation portal, and supporting services.

Information processed

  • generated television activation code;
  • device installation identifier and per-installation credential;
  • television platform, application version, and last-seen time;
  • account label, activation status, and expiration date;
  • playback diagnostics such as content label/type, position, error category, retry count, and recovery result;
  • IP address and security logs required to operate and protect the service.

The application does not require precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access for normal operation.

Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate an installation, activate authorized access, deliver the configured catalog, preserve local playback features, provide support, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

Content catalogs

RonecaPlayTV is a player and activation platform. It does not provide a general commercial media library. Catalog access is configured by an authorized account provider. Official store-review accounts receive a separate demonstration catalog and cannot access customer catalogs.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers solely to provide authentication, hosting, storage, diagnostics, and network delivery, or disclosed when legally required.

Retention and security

Records are retained while needed for the service relationship, support, security, accounting, and legal obligations. We use HTTPS, per-installation credentials, restricted server-side access, role separation, limited review accounts, and audit logs. No system can guarantee absolute security.

User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request information, correction, or deletion through the support contact published in the RonecaPlayTV store listing.

Children

The service is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Account providers are responsible for lawful administration and content suitability.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date shown at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the official support contact displayed on the RonecaPlayTV page in the LG Content Store.
